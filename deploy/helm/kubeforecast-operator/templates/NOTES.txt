{{- if .Values.platform.url }}
Platform-driven scaling is on: the operator reads the auto modules from {{ .Values.platform.url }} every {{ .Values.platform.syncInterval }}
and scales their workloads. Replicas, bounds, fallback and pace are configured on the module in the platform;
no PredictiveScaler object is needed.
{{- end }}

Next: create a Secret with the platform credential and a PredictiveScaler in Observe mode
(see deploy/operator/example-predictivescaler.yaml). Switch it to Auto only after its status shows the decisions you
expect, and only after the system setting forecast.allow_auto_mode has been opened on the platform.

The CRD comes from the crds/ directory of the chart: Helm installs it once and never upgrades or deletes it.
After a chart upgrade that changes the CRD, apply it yourself: kubectl apply -f deploy/operator/crd/
