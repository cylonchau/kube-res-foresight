The kube-res-foresight operator is installed in namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} rollout status deploy/{{ include "kube-res-foresight-operator.fullname" . }}

The operator reads the auto modules from {{ .Values.platform.url }} and scales their workloads; replicas, bounds, fallback and pace
are configured on the module in the platform. It creates no resource of its own in the cluster. Its time limits (request timeout, sync
interval, cache lifetimes) are the system parameters operator.* of the platform.

Modules are scaled only in auto mode, and only after the system setting forecast.allow_auto_mode has been opened on the platform.
