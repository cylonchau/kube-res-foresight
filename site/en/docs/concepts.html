USER GUIDE

Capabilities and concepts

Understand modules, metric templates, models, and operating policies.

Start from the workload

A module describes a Kubernetes workload and its forecasting, resource, and execution policies. Metrics supply inputs, models predict change, and recommendations combine forecasts with constraints. Actual outcomes feed the next evaluation.

Metrics→Forecast and compare→Recommend→Bounded actions→Actual outcomes

Modules, metrics, and models

ConceptWhat it manages
Workload moduleTarget, templates, intervals, thresholds, bounds, mode, and fallback.
Metric templateQueries, variables, meaning, units, and scope, rendered for each module.
DatasetSampling, aggregation, conversion, precision, and gap handling.
ModelCentral activation, parameters, weights, budgets, and adoption thresholds.
Recommendation snapshotResource and replica advice with sources, constraints, version, and expiry.

Four operating modes

  • Observe: collect, forecast, and compare without applying resource changes.
  • Recommend: review resource and replica advice and its reasoning.
  • Admission: apply authorized resource advice to new Pods.
  • Auto: let the Operator adjust replicas within bounds, steps, and cooldowns.

Usage boundaries

The platform manages a single cluster, not cross-cluster migration. Read historical observations, capacity estimates, future forecasts, and actual Ready capacity separately. Estimated per-Pod capacity is not a measured maximum.

Keep one replica controller per workload. Admission affects new Pods; running resource configurations require creation or rolling updates.
