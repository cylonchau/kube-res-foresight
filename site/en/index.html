CAPABILITIES

A connected workflow for every workload

Manage inputs, understand forecasts, bound actions, and keep evaluating actual outcomes.

Workload modules

One workload with its own metrics, intervals, bounds, and fallback.

Models and dynamic selection

Choose a fixed method or dynamic algorithms per module, guided by backtests and actuals.

Look-ahead decisions

Account for readiness time and combine multiple candidates into consensus.

Schedules and fallback

Define business windows and explicit behavior when inputs fail.

Memory and outage protection

Configure OOM protection and bridge outages with time-limited, scale-up-only cached decisions.

Scaling timeliness and audit

Evaluate capacity readiness against actual demand and trace advice through execution.

AI assistant and scheduled analysis

Query state with skills and schedule analyses delivered by email.

Platform activity

Review modules needing attention and applied scaling activity.
