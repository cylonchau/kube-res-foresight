Review platform activity on the home page

Users with home-page access land on the dashboard after login. A shared time window shows enabled and total modules, operating modes, modules needing attention, applied scaling actions, and failed runs.

Modules needing attention

FlagWhat to inspect
Repeated failureAt least three consecutive failures: inspect recent errors, sources, and model runs.
Degraded recommendationReview sources and fallback reasons.
Expired recommendationCheck scheduling, forecasting, and decision refresh.
No recommendationA module expected to produce advice has not done so; review module and metric settings.
BypassConfirm whether manual intervention is intentional.

Observe-only modules and modules with prediction disabled are not flagged solely for missing advice. Bypass may be intentional; read its reason.

Distinguish execution and effectiveness

The scaling chart aggregates successfully applied Operator actions over time, not forecast recommendations. Empty periods show zero actions. Open Effect report from an attention row to inspect accuracy, timeliness, capacity, and stability.

The dashboard reads existing state without starting forecasts, execution, or repairs. Continue with troubleshooting or use the AI assistant for questions.
