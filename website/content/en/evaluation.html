Read a module effect report

Select a module and time window in forecasting results to review resource error, peak underestimation, scale-up timeliness, excess and missing capacity, reversals, degradation reasons, and algorithm adoption. Insufficient or unmeasurable data is identified rather than treated as zero error.

Actual Pod-hours and capacity SLO

The execution and capacity section compares observed Ready replicas with replicas required by measured load. It reports actual, needed, excess, and missing Pod-hours and minutes of insufficient capacity. Data gaps are excluded from compared time.

Capacity SLO is time without a capacity shortfall divided by compared time. Its default target is 99%, configured with forecast.slo_capacity_target_percent. It is not a latency or error-rate SLO; excess Pod-hours are not proven monetary savings. Values require at least 12 compared moments covering at least half the requested window.

It compares forecast-derived replicas without fully replaying cooldowns, steps, look-ahead, or safeguards. Model services use pretrained models rather than background-tuned copies.
