Modes and authorization

Observe and Recommend help build an understanding of workload behavior. Admission and automatic scaling require both global authorization and a suitable module mode. Writes are disabled by default.

Scheduled overrides

Set fixed resources or replicas for defined business windows, including start, end, timezone, priority, and covered fields. Other fields follow their normal policies; normal calculation resumes after the window.

Schedules use explicit IANA timezones and storage stays in UTC. The platform defaults to UTC+8 and pages display UTC+8. Never write converted display times back as storage values.

Degradation and recovery

Each field follows bypass → schedule → eligible model → module fallback → unchanged. Values must remain valid, fresh, and within bounds. OOM floors apply outside bypass.

Record reasons for insufficient history, stale inputs, model failure, or missing eligible forecasts. Consecutive healthy cycles are required before recovery to avoid unstable switching.

When manual intervention is needed

  1. Enable Bypass and record a reason.
  2. Disable the corresponding global setting and return to Recommend or Observe.
  3. Review snapshots, action records, and actual resources.
  4. Fix inputs or policy and compare results before gradually restoring authorization.
Stopping future actions does not undo previous changes

Bypass and global settings do not restore earlier resource or replica configurations automatically. Consider current service state and action history when restoring them.
