Start with one workload

Connect a noncritical workload to your own metrics. Begin with observation, review the advice, and gradually authorize actions.

  1. Add a source. Configure a Metrics data source and check the connection.
  2. Define metric templates. Choose CPU, memory, request volume, or other inputs. Confirm queries, units, and aggregation scope.
  3. Create a module. Select the namespace, workload, and container. Connect templates and configure intervals, bounds, schedules, and fallback.
  4. Run and compare. Inspect history quality, model predictions, backtests, actual performance, and recommendations.

What to review first

AreaWhat to check
History and datasetsScope, units, sample interval, gaps, and freshness.
Model runsSuccessful runs and reasons for insufficient history, missing parameters, or timeout.
Backtests and evaluationCPU and memory forecast errors, particularly peak underestimation.
Recommendation detailsResource and replica advice, constraints, consensus, expiry, and fallback reasons.

Enable automation gradually

Start in Observe and review advice in Recommend. Authorize Admission and Auto separately when ready to apply changes. Both write paths are disabled by global settings by default.

Review before acting

Confirm metric meaning, sufficient history, explainable advice, appropriate bounds, and fallback values. Review admission and replica scaling separately.

Move from collection to model forecasts

Confirm history collection first, then enable prediction. Choose Dynamic algorithms to compare methods, or Fixed algorithm and select a specific method. Non-baseline models need backtest promotion before their results can be adopted.

Fallback defaults are expected while prediction is disabled or No prediction is selected. Stay in Observe or Recommend while reviewing forecasts, scores, and look-ahead details. Authorize admission or scaling separately. See prediction modes.
